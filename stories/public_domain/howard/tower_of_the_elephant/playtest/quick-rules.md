# *The Tower of the Elephant* Playtest Rules

## What you need

Use the paper adventure sheet, a pen, and one twenty-sided die (d20). Two d20s are
convenient when a passage gives Conan Advantage or Disadvantage. Read the
current passage, choose one of its available actions, and follow its
instruction. Some choices lead straight to another passage. Others call for a
test because both success and failure matter.

Record every printed change to Fortune, Stamina, Exposure, possessions, and
story marks before reading the next passage. A choice is available only when
its stated conditions match the sheet. Cross off a possession or story mark
when the passage says it is consumed.

## Conan

You play the young, already seasoned Conan. His profile is fixed:

`BRN 13 | AGI 12 | CUN 12 | WIL 12 | FOR 10/10 | STM 6/6`

There is no character creation. Brawn (BRN) covers force and endurance;
Agility (AGI) covers balance, speed, and physical precision; Cunning (CUN)
covers stealth, observation, and guile; and Will (WIL) covers courage and
self-command. Fortune (FOR) is both a spendable reserve and, when a passage
calls for one, the target of a Fortune test. Stamina (STM) records Conan's
capacity to survive injury. Begin with Fortune 10, Stamina 6, Exposure 0, and
the Sword listed on the adventure sheet.

## Choices and tests

A passage states the stakes before a consequential test: what Conan can gain
and what failure can cost. Choose his method with that knowledge. The method
determines the tested attribute and may grant Advantage or Disadvantage.

Use this sequence:

`state stakes -> choose method -> roll -> select die -> compute margin -> optionally spend Fortune -> apply authored result`

The passage captures the named attribute target when the roll is made. Roll
one d20 unless Advantage or Disadvantage applies. After selecting the die,
Conan succeeds on a result equal to or below the captured attribute. A higher
result fails.

Margin is the captured attribute minus the selected die. A roll of 9 against
CUN 12 has a margin of 3; a roll of 15 has a margin of -3. First compute that
provisional margin. If you spend Fortune, lower the selected die by the amount
spent and recompute the final margin. Then follow the passage's result for the
final die. A passage may use a particular margin only when it says so.

When a passage calls for a Fortune test, use Conan's current Fortune as the
captured target and follow the same roll-under procedure.

## Advantage and Disadvantage

Advantage rolls two d20s and keeps the lower result. Disadvantage rolls two
d20s and keeps the higher result. Select the die before checking for a
natural result, computing margin, or considering Fortune.

Preparation, a suitable method, useful knowledge, or strong position may give
Advantage. A compromised position or poorly suited method may impose
Disadvantage. The passage tells you when either applies; do not add numerical
modifiers of your own.

## Natural 1 and natural 20

A selected natural 1 is locked. Follow the passage's exceptional natural-1
result, even when an ordinary success would have led to the same destination.

A selected natural 20 is also locked. Follow the worst credible result printed
for that test. When the stated danger is mortal, this can kill Conan
immediately.

Neither a natural 1 nor a natural 20 can be changed with Fortune.

## Fortune

After seeing an ordinary selected die, you may spend Fortune to lower it.
Lowering the die by one costs 1 Fortune; lowering it by four costs 4 Fortune.
The Fortune cost is always equal to the number of points by which the selected
die is lowered. You may not spend more Fortune than Conan currently has.

Choose the spend, lower the selected die, and determine the result against the
target captured when the roll was made. Deduct the cost after that captured
test resolves, then record the new current Fortune. This timing matters during
a Fortune test: paying the cost does not change the target already captured
for that roll.

Fortune can turn an ordinary failure into a success or deepen a success when a
passage explicitly reads margin. It cannot change either locked natural
result. Fortune never rises above 10, and no Fortune recovery occurs within
this slice.

## Stamina

Passages inflict Stamina loss directly. Cross off the printed amount: 1 point
for an ordinary injury in this slice or 2 points for a serious injury. Some
clearly stated mortal hazards can send Conan straight to the death ending
without first reducing Stamina.

If an authored damaging result reduces Conan to 0 Stamina, go immediately to
`E03`; Conan dies. There is no rescue roll or automatic rebound. Stamina never
rises above 6, and no Stamina recovery occurs within this slice.

## Exposure

Exposure measures evidence, delay, and disturbance that make the intrusion
more likely to be discovered. It starts at 0, is visible, and is capped at 5.
Apply each printed increase before continuing.

| Exposure | State and effect |
| ---: | --- |
| 0-2 | The intruders remain unconfirmed. All listed choices remain available. |
| 3 | The watch has reason for unease. Passages acknowledge movement or attention below, and lingering choices close. |
| 4 | A search is beginning. Only immediate advance or withdrawal choices remain legal. |
| 5 | The breach is discovered. Resolve the contextual `E02` crisis and do not reduce Exposure to preserve the route. |

Exposure increases only when the adventure prints one of these events:

- add 1 when Conan deliberately waits through another patrol interval;
- add 1 when he calls loudly or creates an avoidable disturbance;
- add 1 for an ordinary failed standoff with Taurus;
- add 2 for a natural 20 during that standoff; or
- add the amount printed by the selected lion tactic on a costly result.

No ordinary event lowers Exposure in this slice. If an update reaches 5, go to
the contextual `E02` ending instead of continuing along the former route.

## Tactical exchanges

A fight is resolved through authored choices and consequences. There are no
enemy statistics or repeated combat rounds. Each exchange presents a specific
situation, asks how Conan responds, and uses at most one player-facing test.
The passage prints the effect on Conan and any Stamina or Exposure cost.

Possessions and story marks can unlock a method. If a method consumes one,
cross it off when instructed. During the silent-lion exchange, read the fatal
stake first, choose among the methods currently available, make the stated
test, apply Fortune only to an ordinary selected die, and resolve every
printed cost before leaving the passage.

## Endings

Terminal passages end this playtest route explicitly. `E01` means Conan
withdraws alive and abandons the theft. `E02` means the watch discovers the
breach at Exposure 5. `E03` means Conan dies. `S14` is the successful end of
this infiltration slice, with Taurus's rope ready for the ascent; it is not
the end of the full story.

When you reach any of these terminals, stop. Do not seek an unprinted rescue,
recovery, or continuation. No recovery of Fortune, Stamina, or Exposure occurs
within this slice.
