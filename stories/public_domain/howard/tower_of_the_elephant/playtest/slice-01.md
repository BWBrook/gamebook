# *The Tower of the Elephant*: Outer Wall Playtest

Read the [quick rules](quick-rules.md), copy or print the
[adventure sheet](adventure-sheet.md), and begin at **S01**.

## S01 — The Outer Wall

Thick shrubbery hides you from the empty streets of the temple quarter. Above
it rises the outer wall of Yara's precinct, high enough to stop an ordinary
thief. You measure the pale stone with your eyes. A leap will put your fingers
on the coping; from there, your own strength will do the rest.

Beyond that wall stands a second one, and beyond the second gleams the silver
tower. Its jewel-crusted rim catches the starlight. No lamp burns in its smooth
height. Men say that Yara has lived for centuries by the power of the Heart of
the Elephant. They also tell how he made a hostile prince shrivel into a black
spider beneath the rays of an evil gem. Such tales are the breath of cowards,
yet the hair along your neck rises.

Then comes a measured tread from the garden. Steel gives a faint clink. A
guard walks within, though no patrol was visible from the street.

- Climb now, while you know where the guard is. Turn to **S03**.

- Wait for the measured tread to pass again. Add **1 Exposure**, then turn to
  **S02**.

## S02 — The Broken Interval

You press against the cold stone and listen. The measured tread passes out of
hearing. You count the heartbeats until the guard should return.

He does not.

The silence is deeper for that absence. Somewhere beyond the wall, a fountain
tinkles. The next patrol interval is already approaching, and the delay you
have taken will soon become evidence that someone watched this place too
closely. There is no profit in waiting through another round.

Leap for the coping and cross the wall. Turn to **S03**.

## S03 — The Dead Guard

You catch the coping, swing up with one arm, and lie flat upon the broad stone.
Starlight exposes the even sward between the walls. Trimmed bushes stand near
the inner barrier; the small fountain is the only thing that speaks.

You lower yourself inside, draw your sword, and run crouched through the naked
light toward the bushes. A crumpled body almost takes your feet from under
you. The man wears the silvered armour and crested helmet of the royal guard.
His shield and spear lie beside him. His flesh is warm. This is the man whose
tread you heard, dead within moments and without a cry.

A branch shifts near the inner wall. You catch the suggestion of a heavy shape
moving behind the leaves.

- Follow the movement at once, before its maker escapes or takes a stronger
  position. Turn to **S05**.

- Spend time on one close examination of the corpse. Turn to **S04**.

## S04 — One Discovery

The unknown mover is still near. You have time to take one useful thing from
the dead man, no more. Choose exactly one discovery; do not record both.

- Take up the source-backed weapon lying beside the body. Add **Guard's
  spear** to your Possessions. Its shaft is long enough to brace against a
  charging beast. Turn to **S05**.

- Leave the spear and read the corpse instead. The throat bears the bruising
  of powerful hands, and the position of the body shows a swift attack from
  behind rather than sorcery. Add **Read the strangling** to your Story marks.
  Turn to **S05**.

## S05 — A Thief in the Shrubs

You move toward the inner wall. A huge human shape waits close to the stone,
quiet enough to have killed the guard and heard your own careful advance. The
stranger has not yet shown his purpose. How you break the standoff depends on
what you learned at the corpse.

If you have **Read the strangling**, the marks on the dead man have already
given you the stranger's measure. You name him softly as a human ambusher and
fellow thief before either of you lunges. Cross off **Read the strangling**.
The quiet recognition costs no Stamina or Exposure. Turn to **S06**.

If you do not have **Read the strangling**, choose one of the following
methods.

- Call from the edge of the shrubs that you know a man is hiding there. The
  challenge forces an answer, but your voice carries across the open sward.
  Add **1 Exposure**. If Exposure is now 5, turn to **E02**. Otherwise, turn
  to **S06**.

- Stalk the stranger and try to seize the better position. Read all the stakes
  below before rolling.

  **Method:** Glide through the leaves as silently as a hunting panther and
  come upon the stranger from his blind side.

  **Test:** Roll one d20 against **CUN 12**. This is a standard roll, with
  neither Advantage nor Disadvantage.

  **Success:** You reach the stranger without cost and turn to **S06**.

  **Ordinary failure:** The stranger seizes the position first. Lose **1
  Stamina**, add **1 Exposure**, and turn to **S06** unless a crisis instruction
  below sends you elsewhere.

  **Natural 1:** You take the superior position so completely that the
  stranger checks his attack before it begins. Take no cost and turn to
  **S06**.

  **Natural 20:** The stranger catches you at full disadvantage before either
  thief understands the other. Lose **2 Stamina**, add **2 Exposure**, and
  turn to **S06** unless a crisis instruction below sends you elsewhere.

  **Fortune:** After an ordinary selected die, you may spend Fortune point for
  point to lower it. You may not alter a natural 1 or natural 20.

  Now roll. Resolve a selected natural 1 or natural 20 first. On any ordinary
  die, decide whether to spend Fortune, then compare the final result with CUN
  12. Apply the announced result. If Stamina reaches 0, turn immediately to
  **E03**. Otherwise, if Exposure reaches 5, turn immediately to **E02**. If
  neither crisis occurs, turn to **S06**.

## S06 — The Prince of Thieves

The shape wheels with startling speed. A thick pair of hands starts forward,
then halts when your blade catches the starlight. The man is as tall as you
and heavier, big-bellied yet balanced like a wrestler. A thin, knotted rope
hangs coiled across him. His bare feet make no sound.

He sees that you wear no royal silver. You see that he is no soldier.

“A thief,” he whispers.

“Name yourself,” you answer, keeping the sword between you.

He calls himself Taurus of Nemedia. You know the name: thieves speak it with
the respect princes demand from courtiers. When you give your own name and
say that you have come for Yara's Heart, his belly shakes with a low laugh.
There is no mockery in it. Taurus had believed that he alone possessed the
courage to attempt this theft. He has prepared for months; you came upon a
sudden purpose. Each recognises in the other something rarer than caution.

You ask whether he killed the guard. Taurus admits it readily. He crossed
while the patrol was elsewhere, waited in the bushes, and closed his hands
around the man's throat when the fellow blundered near.

“You left him exposed,” you say. “That is a mistake.”

Anger flashes in Taurus's eyes. Then he explains the harder calculation. The
guard will not be changed until after midnight. If searchers find the body,
they will run to Yara with the news and leave the thieves a chance to escape;
if they find nothing, they will beat through every bush until they trap the
intruders. You consider this and concede the point.

Taurus's pride settles. He has studied the inner garden and prepared an upper
route past the soldiers below. The unseen sentinels inside are deadlier than
men, but he claims to have their answer.

Hear the alliance he offers. Turn to **S07**.

## S07 — A Bargain Between Thieves

Taurus says he has never shared an adventure, but he will share this one. He
means to enter from the top, descend upon Yara, and take the Heart before the
sorcerer can work one of his strange dooms. He promises no safety. A good
thief, he says, knows when wealth is worth the risk.

If your Exposure is 5, the choice has already been taken from you. Turn
immediately to **E02**.

At Exposure 3, a faint scrape of metal beyond the precinct tells you the
watch has grown uneasy. At Exposure 4, muted movement gathers into the
beginning of a search. In either case there is no time for more talk: you may
advance at once or withdraw at once.

- Accept Taurus as an ally and match your courage against his preparation.
  Turn to **S08**.

- Abandon this attempt while the walls still offer a way out. Turn to
  **E01**.

## S08 — The Inner Wall

“I will go as far as any man,” you tell Taurus.

You slip off your sandals. Bare feet give you the stone and grass beneath your
soles. Taurus turns to the inner wall,
leaps, catches the coping, and draws his great bulk upward with a suppleness
that would shame a lean acrobat. You follow. This ordinary climb asks no test
of either thief.

From the coping you see no light and no opening in the gleaming lower tower.
Taurus says its doors and windows are cunningly fitted and now shut; the
soldiers within take their air from above.

If your Exposure is 5, discovery overtakes the crossing. Turn immediately to
**E02**. At Exposure 3, distant movement tells you the uneasy watch has begun
to attend to the silence. At Exposure 4, a search is forming. Taurus cuts off
all further observation and signals you down at once.

Cross with Taurus into the inner garden. Turn to **S09**.

## S09 — Eyes in the Garden

The inner garden is a pool of starlit shadow. Feathery bushes and low trees
move against a breeze too weak to cool your skin. Something old in your blood
recognises a hunting scent. The short hairs on your neck bristle.

Taurus draws a slender copper tube from his girdle. He orders you to follow
and keep behind him if you value your life. When you drop to the sward, he
pushes you back against the wall and waits. A shrub shakes though the breeze
has died. Two great eyes burn within it; then other pairs kindle around them.

Five lions are visible. There may be more behind the leaves. They move without
the clamour of common beasts, low rumbles buried in their chests.

If your Exposure is 5, the outer watch discovers the breach before Taurus can
act. Turn immediately to **E02**. At Exposure 3 or 4, sounds beyond the garden
say the watch is stirring, and the danger ahead leaves no interval to study or
debate.

Trust Taurus's command. Keep behind him and give him room to use the copper
tube. Turn to **S10**.

## S10 — The Black Lotus

Taurus steps as if every blade of grass were sharp steel. The lions glide
closer. You grip your sword and wait for the charge, but Taurus raises the
tube to his mouth and blows. Yellow powder streams outward and swells into a
thick green-yellow cloud. It swallows the fiery eyes. Taurus springs back to
the wall.

He names the cloud simply: death. The powder comes from the black lotus of
distant Khitai. One breath can kill a man. If the wind turns, both thieves
must flee the garden; the air remains still. Competence means waiting where
Taurus placed you until the visible poison disperses, so make no roll and
recover no Fortune or Stamina.

If your Exposure is 5, the delay has become discovery. Turn immediately to
**E02**. At Exposure 3, the watch's unease can be heard as brief, uncertain
movement beyond the walls. At Exposure 4, the search has begun. You still
must not enter the lethal cloud, but you lose every lingering option and move
the instant the last yellow shred vanishes.

When the air clears, five tawny bodies lie among the shrubs. The lions have
died without a cry. A sweet, cloying scent remains. Taurus says the powder is
gone; obtaining that single charge was itself a feat worthy of his fame, and
he has none left for the soldiers in the tower.

Advance with him as soon as the poison has dispersed. Turn to **S11**.

## S11 — The Lion That Made No Sound

You and Taurus glide through the shrubs to the silver tower. He unwinds his
knotted rope and shows the steel hook at its end. While he begins to swing it
for the cast, you listen against the smooth wall. No sound comes from the
soldiers within. The intrusion may still be unknown.

Then your savage instinct tears you around. A sixth lion rises against the
stars at killing range. It escaped the lotus cloud, and it attacks without
roar, growl, or warning.

Read the common fatal stake and all three methods before choosing:

**Fatal stake:** Whatever method you choose, the lion is already close enough
to kill. A selected natural 20 is locked and sends you directly to **E03**.
It cannot be changed with Fortune. A damaging ordinary failure can also send
you to **E03** if it reduces Stamina to 0, or to **E02** if it raises Exposure
to 5 while you remain alive.

| Method | Availability and roll | Clean success | Ordinary failure | Natural 1 | Natural 20 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brace the guard's spear | Requires **Guard's spear**. Cross it off before rolling. Roll **BRN 13 with Advantage**: two d20s, keep the lower. | The lion dies cleanly; turn to **S12**. | Lose **2 Stamina**, add **1 Exposure**, then turn to **S13** if no crisis intervenes. | You kill it before it can make a sound; turn to **S12**. | The lion kills you; turn to **E03**. |
| Wheel beneath the leap | Always available. Use your Sword. Roll **BRN 13**, one d20. | The lion dies cleanly; turn to **S12**. | Lose **2 Stamina**, add **1 Exposure**, then turn to **S13** if no crisis intervenes. | You split its skull cleanly; turn to **S12**. | The lion kills you; turn to **E03**. |

Roll **AGI 12**, one d20. | The lion dies cleanly; turn to **S12**. | Lose **1 Stamina**, add **2 Exposure**, then turn to **S13** if no crisis intervenes. | You turn its own speed against it; turn to **S12**. | The lion kills you; turn to **E03**. |

**Fortune:** Every method permits Fortune after an ordinary selected die.
Spend Fortune point for point to lower that die before determining success.
Neither a selected natural 1 nor a selected natural 20 can be altered.

Choose one available method and roll in its printed mode. Resolve a selected
natural result first. For an ordinary die, decide whether to spend Fortune,
then compare the final result with the named target. On success, turn to
**S12**. On failure, apply the exact Stamina and Exposure costs for your
method. If Stamina reaches 0, turn immediately to **E03**. Otherwise, if
Exposure reaches 5, turn immediately to **E02**. If neither crisis occurs,
turn to **S13**.

## S12 — The Clean Kill

The beast comes without a sound, but your chosen stroke is quicker. Tawny
weight and driving muscle collapse beside you. Whether spear or sword dealt
the blow, the lion dies before it can voice an alarm.

Taurus stares from the rope to the carcass and then to you. Astonishment
breaks through the confidence of the prince of thieves. He has seen nimble
killers in every thieves' quarter of Zamora and beyond, yet the speed of this
touch-and-go slaying leaves him briefly without words.

You wrench your weapon free. If you used the Sword, you cleanse its blade on
the grass. You have taken no Stamina or Exposure cost from this exchange.

Give Taurus room to finish the cast. Turn to **S14**.

## S13 — Victory at a Cost

The lion dies beneath your final stroke, but the struggle is neither clean nor
quiet. Claws or crushing weight tear you before the carcass falls, and the
brief violence scuffs the garden's stillness. Taurus hauls the dead weight
away while you force yourself upright.

You have already applied in **S11** the exact cost printed for your chosen
method; do not apply it a second time here. Confirm the result before
continuing. If your Stamina is 0, turn immediately to **E03**. If you remain
alive but your Exposure is 5, turn immediately to **E02**.

With Stamina above 0 and Exposure below 5, keep your weapon in hand and give
Taurus room to cast. Turn to **S14**.

## S14 — The Rope Holds

Taurus resumes the interrupted swing and releases the hook with a smooth snap
of his mighty arm. Steel curves into the darkness above the jeweled rim. Luck
holds on the first cast: the line draws taut. He tests it first with cautious
jerks, then with the full pull of his weight. Nothing slips.

The silent lion lies dead. The five others remain in the lotus-scented
shadows. Taurus declares that the cord can bear three times his weight and
will climb first. The route upward is secure.

**Slice complete.** This is the successful boundary of the outer-wall and
garden playtest, not an ending to the full adventure. The full story would
continue upward from this point, but that continuation is not printed here.

Retain the exact final state on your adventure sheet for a later continuation:

- your current **Fortune**;
- your current **Stamina**;
- your current **Exposure**;
- every uncrossed **Possession**, including the Sword and the **Guard's
  spear** only if you recorded it and did not consume it at S11; and
- every uncrossed **Story mark**. **Read the strangling** is crossed off when
  consumed at S05.

Recover nothing and erase nothing.

## E01 — The Theft Abandoned

**Ending:** You choose survival over this attempt.

The walls are still behind you rather than around you. You leave Taurus to his
prepared road and cross back into the temple-quarter shadows. Pride stings,
but no jewel is worth pretending that another man's plan is your own.

You remain alive, but you do not take the Heart of the Elephant. Taurus and
the upper route pass out of your story, and this choice stands outside the
canonical theft. The attempt is over.

## E02 — The Breach Discovered

**Ending:** Exposure has reached 5, and the watch discovers the intrusion.

Voices answer the evidence you have left. Steel sounds within the lower tower
and beyond the garden wall; the warning will run to Yara, just as Taurus said
it would. With the lotus powder spent and the upper route exposed, no quiet
breach remains.

You abandon the tower and win clear while escape is still possible. Yara is
warned, Taurus's prepared approach is lost, and this attempt at the Heart ends
as a known failure. There is no reset or second try.

## E03 — Death in the Garden

**Ending:** Conan dies during the breach.

If a damaging result reduced Stamina to 0, blood and shock take the strength
from you before the precinct can be crossed. If the silent lion's selected die
was a natural 20, its noiseless rush reaches your throat before strength,
speed, or Fortune can answer it.

The tower remains above the dark garden, and the Heart remains beyond your
reach.
