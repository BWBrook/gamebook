# *The Tower of the Elephant*: Outer Wall Playtest

Read the [player's rules](quick-rules.md) first. Then copy or print the
[Adventure Sheet](adventure-sheet.md), fill in Conan's starting values, and
begin at **S01**.

## S01 — The Outer Wall

Thick shrubbery hugs the foot of Yara's outer wall, hiding you from the silent
streets of the temple quarter. You creep close and measure the pale barrier
with your eyes. It is high enough to turn back an ordinary thief. A leap will
put your fingers on the coping; from there it is child's play to swing yourself
up and over.

Beyond this wall rises a second, and beyond the second the shimmering shaft of
the silver tower climbs frostily into the stars: a hundred and fifty feet of
smooth, windowless metal crowned with a rim crusted in great jewels. No lamp
burns anywhere in its height. The hair along your neck prickles as you recall
the drunken page's tale of Yara: how the ancient high priest laughed in the
face of a hostile prince and held up a glowing, evil gem; how blinding rays
shot from it until the prince screamed, withered into a blackened lump, and
scampered wildly as a black spider until Yara set his heel upon him. The very
king of Zamora drinks himself stuporous for fear of the sorcerer, who is said
to live for centuries by the power of that gem, the Heart of the Elephant.

Then you shrink back against the stone. Within the garden someone is passing
with a measured stride. Steel gives a faint clink. After all, a guard does pace
those gardens.

- Climb now, while you know where the guard is. Turn to **S03**.

- Press against the stone and wait for him to pass again. Add **1 Exposure**,
  then turn to **S02**.

## S02 — The Broken Round

You crouch motionless in the shrubs, measuring the patrol's round against the
steady throb of your own blood. He should pass this stretch of wall again soon.

He does not. Silence settles thick over the mysterious gardens. Somewhere
within, a fountain tinkles, and nothing else stirs. A soldier of the royal
guard does not abandon his beat in mid-round. Either he has turned back, or
something in that starlit dark has stopped him—and stopped him without a cry.

You have spent time you may come to grudge, but you cross the wall knowing what
a hastier thief would not: whatever waits inside kills without a sound. Write
**Warned by the silence** under Story marks. Then leap for the coping and turn
to **S03**.

## S03 — The Dead Guard

Leaping lightly, you catch the coping, swing up with one arm, and lie flat on
the broad stone. Starlight pours over the wide, even sward between the walls;
carefully trimmed bushes crouch near the inner barrier, and somewhere a
fountain tinkles in the stillness.

You lower yourself inside and draw your sword. The nervousness of the wild
pricks your skin at standing exposed in that naked light. Hugging the curve of
the wall's shadow until you are abreast of the shrubbery, you run crouched
across the grass and almost trip over a crumpled form at the edge of the
bushes.

A swift glance right and left shows no enemy in sight. The dead man wears the
silvered armour and crested helmet of the Zamorian royal guard; his shield and
spear lie beside him, and his flesh is still warm. This is the soldier whose
tread you heard, struck down within moments and without a cry.

Straining your eyes in the gloom, you catch a hint of motion through the shrubs
against the inner wall. Something heavy is shifting behind the leaves.

- Go after it now, before it can slip away or ready itself. You leave the
  corpse unread and the spear where it lies, but you will catch the mover while
  its mind is still on the body you found. Turn to **S05**.

- Spend a heartbeat on the corpse first. Whatever waits in the bushes will have
  time to hear you and brace itself, though the dead man may yield one thing
  worth taking. Turn to **S04**.

## S04 — One Discovery

Leaves whisper again near the inner wall. You have a single heartbeat before
whatever lurks there melts away or leaps out upon you: time for one act, no
more. Choose one; do not record both.

- Snatch up the guard's spear. Its stout ash shaft is long enough to brace
  against a charging beast. Write **Guard's spear** under Possessions. Turn to
  **S05**.

- Leave the spear and read the body instead. Great bruises ring the throat, and
  the sprawl of the limbs tells of a swift grip from behind: no Zamorian
  sorcery, no beast, but a man's terrible hands. Write **Read the strangling**
  under Story marks. Turn to **S05**.

## S05 — A Thief in the Shrubs

You glide toward the inner wall. Close against the stone crouches a huge bulk,
dimly seen in the shadow of the leaves—human, to your wild relief, but still
and watchful enough to have choked the life from a mail-clad guard without a
sound.

**If you have Read the strangling**, the dead soldier has already told you what
kind of wolf hides here: a man, a strangler, and no soldier of Yara. You call
him softly for what he is, a thief, before either of you lunges, holding your
naked steel where the starlight strikes it. Cross off **Read the strangling**
and turn to **S06**.

Otherwise, choose one.

- Call low from the edge of the shrubs that you know a man hides there. Your
  challenge checks any ambush, though the voice carries across the open sward.
  The big shape freezes, then wheels to face your blade. Add **1 Exposure** and
  turn to **S06**.

- Stalk him. You make no more noise than a panther stealing through the night,
  coming upon him from his blind side. Test **Cunning 12**. Roll with
  **Advantage** if you have **Warned by the silence**, or if you came straight
  from **S03** without stopping at the corpse. Cross off the mark if you used
  it.

  *Success:* he hears you only at the last heartbeat and wheels with a gasp,
  hands clutching, then recoils as your blade flashes frostily in the
  starlight. Turn to **S06**.

  *Failure:* he hears you too soon and plunges out of the leaves like a
  cornered bear; knuckles crack and steel scrapes in a brief, savage scuffle
  before you wrench clear and hold him at sword's point. Lose **1 Stamina** and
  add **1 Exposure**. Turn to **S06**.

  *Natural 1:* you are upon him like a shadow out of the Cimmerian hills; your
  point rests against his ribs before his hands can even rise. Turn to **S06**.

  *Natural 20:* he catches you off stride in the gloom, huge hands seizing your
  sword-arm and slamming you hard against the stone before you tear loose and
  bring steel between you. Lose **2 Stamina** and add **2 Exposure**. Turn to
  **S06**.

## S06 — The Prince of Thieves

For a tense instant neither of you speaks, standing poised in the starlight
with bare steel between you. The stranger is as tall as you and heavier,
big-bellied and fat, yet every shift of his bare feet carries a supple, dynamic
grace, and keen eyes glint vitally in the gloom. A coil of thin, knotted rope
hangs across his chest.

“You are no soldier,” he hisses at last. “You are a thief like myself.”

“And who are you?” you answer in a suspicious whisper.

“Taurus of Nemedia.”

You lower your point. “I've heard of you. Men call you a prince of thieves.”

A low laugh answers you. When you name yourself Conan of Cimmeria and tell him
you have come for Yara's jewel, his great belly shakes in silent mirth, with no
mockery in it.

“By Bel, god of thieves!” he hisses. “I had thought only myself had courage to
attempt that poaching. These Zamorians call themselves thieves—bah! I've had my
plans laid for months, and you, I think, have acted on a sudden impulse. Conan,
I like your grit.”

“You killed the soldier?” you ask.

“Of course,” he admits readily. He slid over the wall while the man paced the
far side, hid in the bushes, and when the half-blind fool came blundering over,
slipped behind him and choked out his life. “A good thief should have eyes like
a cat.”

“You made one mistake,” you say. “You should have dragged the body into the
bushes.”

Anger flashes in his eyes. “I? I, a mistake? Impossible!” Then he speaks with
the scorn of a master instructing a novice. The guard is not changed until past
midnight. Should searchers come now and stumble on the body, they will flee
bellowing to Yara and give you time to escape. Should they find nothing, they
will beat through every bush and catch you both like rats in a trap.

“You are right,” you agree.

Taurus's pride settles. “So. Now attend. There are no human guards in the inner
garden, though there are sentinels even more deadly, and I have found a way to
circumvent them.” Turn to **S07**.

## S07 — A Bargain Between Thieves

“I never shared an adventure with anyone,” Taurus whispers, “but by Bel, we'll
attempt this together if you're willing.”

He means to scale the top of the tower, steal down upon old Yara, and strangle
the sorcerer before he can cast any of his accursed spells. He makes no hollow
promises. “It's the chance of being turned into a spider or a toad, against the
wealth and power of the world. All good thieves must know how to take risks.”

- Go as far as any man. Accept Taurus as an ally and turn to **S08**.

- Leave this theft to the man who planned it, while the outer wall is still at
  your back. Turn to **E01**.

## S08 — The Inner Wall

“I'll go as far as any man,” you tell him, slipping off your sandals.

“Then follow me.” Taurus turns, leaps, catches the inner wall, and draws his
great bulk upward with an uncanny suppleness, seeming almost to glide over the
coping. You follow, and lying flat on the broad stone, the two of you speak in
wary whispers.

“I see no light,” you mutter. The lower tower rises like the shaft above: a
perfect, gleaming cylinder without a visible opening.

“There are cleverly constructed doors and windows,” Taurus answers, “but they
are closed. The soldiers inside breathe air that comes from above.”

Below you the garden lies a vague pool of shadows, where feathery bushes and
low spreading trees wave darkly in the starlight. Your wary soul feels the aura
of waiting menace brooding over it. You sense the burning glare of unseen eyes
and catch a subtle wild scent that makes the short hairs on your neck bristle
as a hunting dog bristles at an ancient enemy.

Drop down after Taurus. Turn to **S09**.

## S09 — Eyes in the Garden

Taurus draws a slender copper tube from his girdle. “Follow me,” he whispers,
“and keep behind me, as you value your life.” He drops lightly to the sward
inside the wall. You are close behind with sword ready, but he thrusts you back
against the stone and holds rigid, his gaze locked on the shadowy mass of
shrubbery a few yards away.

The shrubbery shakes, though the breeze has died. Two great eyes blaze out of
the waving gloom, and behind them other sparks of fire kindle in the dark.

“Lions!” you mutter.

“Aye. By day they are kept in subterranean caverns below the tower. That is why
there are no guards in this garden.”

You count five pairs of eyes in sight, and there may be more back in the
bushes. Low rumblings roll from the shadows and the blazing eyes glide forward.
You can sense the great slavering jaws, the tufted tails lashing tawny sides.
The air tightens; your hand locks on the hilt, bracing for the irresistible
hurtling of giant bodies.

Hold where Taurus put you and give him room. Turn to **S10**.

## S10 — The Black Lotus

“Be silent!” Taurus hisses. He steps out from the wall as if treading on
razors, lifts the slender copper tube to his lips, and blows powerfully. A long
jet of yellowish powder shoots from the far end and billows instantly into a
thick green-yellow cloud that settles over the shrubbery, blotting out the
glaring eyes. He runs swiftly back to the wall.

From the cloud comes no sound at all.

“What is that mist?” you ask uneasily.

“Death!” hisses the Nemedian. “If a wind springs up and blows it back upon us,
we must flee over the wall. But the air is still, and now it is dissipating.
Wait until it vanishes entirely. To breathe it is death.”

So you wait, and the stillness stretches taut. If your Exposure is 3 or higher,
a door grates somewhere beyond the outer wall while you stand there, and
Taurus's head snaps toward the sound: the watch has begun to wonder about its
missing man.

Presently only yellowish shreds hang ghostily in the air; then they too are
gone, and Taurus motions you forward. Stretched in the shadows lie five great
tawny shapes, the fire of their grim eyes dimmed for ever. A sweetish, cloying
scent lingers over them.

“They died without a sound!” you mutter.

It was powder of the black lotus, Taurus tells you, whose blossoms wave in the
lost jungles of Khitai, where only the yellow-skulled priests of Yun dwell. He
stole that single cloth-of-gold bag from a caravan bound for Stygia, lifting it
out of the coils of the great serpent that guarded it without waking the beast,
a feat enough to make any thief's name. That one charge was all he possessed;
none remains for the soldiers in the tower. “Come, in Bel's name! Are we to
waste the night in discussion?”

Follow him to the foot of the tower. Turn to **S11**.

## S11 — The Lion That Made No Sound

You glide through the shrubbery to the gleaming foot of the tower. With a
gesture enjoining silence, Taurus unwinds his knotted cord, a strong steel hook
fastened to one end, and begins to swing it about his head. You lay your ear to
the smooth silver wall and hear nothing. The soldiers inside suspect no more
than the night wind blowing through the trees, though a strange nervousness
prickles your blood, heavy with the smell of lions.

Then your savage instinct tears you around, for the death that is upon you
makes no sound. A giant tawny shape rears upright against the stars, towering
over you for the death-stroke. A sixth lion escaped the black lotus, and it
neither roars nor growls as it strikes.

It is already at killing range. Whatever method you choose, a natural 20 is
death, and Fortune cannot save you. On an ordinary roll you may spend Fortune
as usual.

- **Brace the guard's spear.** Only if you have **Guard's spear**. Cross it
  off now; the brace commits the weapon whatever follows. Test **Brawn 13
  with Advantage**.

  *Success:* the great beast impales itself upon the iron point and crashes
  lifeless at your feet. Turn to **S12**.

  *Failure:* the ash shaft splinters beneath its rush and claws rake you as the
  beast drives through. Lose **2 Stamina**, add **1 Exposure**, and turn to
  **S13**.

  *Natural 1:* the point drives deep before the lion can loose a sound; it dies
  in mid-leap. Turn to **S12**.

  *Natural 20:* the iron skates from its slanting skull and its whole murderous
  weight comes down upon you. Turn to **E03**.

- **Meet the leap with your sword.** Throw every ounce of desperate nerve and
  thew behind one stroke. Test **Brawn 13**.

  *Success:* your sword flashes frostily in the starlight, splits its slanting
  skull in half, and man and beast go down together. You heave yourself from
  beneath the limp carcass. Turn to **S12**.

  *Failure:* your steel bites deep, but the lion's claws and bulk crush you to
  the grass as it dies. Lose **2 Stamina**, add **1 Exposure**, and turn to
  **S13**.

  *Natural 1:* no civilised man could move half so fast; your blade splits its
  skull before the beast reaches the crest of its leap. Turn to **S12**.

  *Natural 20:* the silent beast is quicker than your steel. Turn to **E03**.

- **Slip aside and cut as it passes.** Let its own blind rush carry it past
  your edge. Test **Agility 12**.

  *Success:* you sway clear of the sweeping claws and open its flank as it
  hurtles by; it crashes lifeless into the shrubs behind you. Turn to **S12**.

  *Failure:* a heavy paw clips you as it drives past, hurling you both crashing
  through the bushes. Lose **1 Stamina**, add **2 Exposure**, and turn to
  **S13**.

  *Natural 1:* you pivot like a hill panther, turning its own terrible speed
  against your blade. Turn to **S12**.

  *Natural 20:* it is not fooled; the tawny bulk twists in mid-leap and bears
  you down. Turn to **E03**.

## S12 — The Clean Kill

The beast came without a sound, but your stroke was swifter still. Tawny muscle
and driving weight collapse beside you before the lion can voice an alarm.

Cursing incoherently beneath his breath, Taurus stares from the carcass to you,
bewildered by the stunning swiftness of that touch-and-go instant. He has seen
quick killers in every thieves' quarter from Nemedia to Zamora, and never one
who moved like that.

“Are you hurt, man?” he gasps.

“No, by Crom!” you grunt. If you braced the spear, its shaft lies splintered or
its iron buried past recovery; leave it. If you used your sword, you wrench it
free and cleanse the dripping steel on the grass.

“All things are strange in this garden,” Taurus mutters. “The lions strike
silently—and so do other deaths.” Give him room to finish the cast. Turn to
**S14**.

## S13 — Victory at a Cost

The lion dies beneath your final stroke, its skull cloven, but not cleanly and
not quietly. Claws and crushing weight have torn you before the carcass went
limp, and the struggle has broken the garden's hush. Cursing beneath his
breath, Taurus lays hold of the dead beast and helps you thrust it aside as you
clamber upright, still gripping your dripping sword.

Check your sheet. If your Stamina is 0, turn to **E03**. If your Exposure is
5, turn to **E02**.

Otherwise, Taurus gasps out, “Are you hurt, man?” You tell him it was as close
a call as you have had in a life noways tame. He glances up at the gleaming
silver shaft. Little sound was made in the slaying, though the soldiers inside
might have heard, if they are not asleep or drunk. Ready your blade and give
him room to cast. Turn to **S14**.

## S14 — The Rope Holds

Taurus resumes his swing and casts the line with a smooth, ripping motion of
his mighty arm. The steel hook curves upward and inward and vanishes over the
jewelled rim. Cautious jerking, then a hard haul with all his weight: nothing
slips or gives.

“Luck the first cast,” he murmurs.

The silent lion lies dead; the five others stretch motionless in the
lotus-scented shadows. If your Exposure is 3 or higher, a voice lifts somewhere
beyond the outer wall and breaks off short: the watch is hunting its missing
man, and less of the night remains to you.

“We must climb this cord,” Taurus says. “Little need to ask a Cimmerian if he
can.”

“If it will bear my weight,” you grunt.

“It will bear thrice my own. It was woven from the tresses of dead women, which
I took from their tombs at midnight, and steeped in the deadly wine of the upas
tree, to give it strength. I will go first—then follow me closely.”

**This part of the adventure is complete.** The story continues up the rope,
but that continuation is not printed here. Keep your Adventure Sheet exactly
as it stands, including your Exposure, any possession you still hold, and any
story mark you have not crossed off. Nothing recovers, and nothing is erased.

## E01 — The Theft Abandoned

Taurus shrugs and turns toward the inner wall without a backward glance. You
climb back over the outer coping and drop into the empty shadows of the temple
quarter. Pride stings bitter in your throat, but no gem is worth walking
another man's road into a sorcerer's tower.

You are alive, and the Heart of the Elephant rests untouched in its silver
height. Whether Taurus of Nemedia reaches it, you will not learn tonight. The
adventure ends here.

## E02 — The Breach Discovered

Shouts ring out beyond the outer wall, answered by the sudden rasp of steel and
slamming metal inside the tower's lower hall. Word is running straight to Yara,
just as Taurus warned. With the black lotus spent and the upper climb blown, no
quiet road into the tower remains.

You win clear over the walls while darkness still covers your retreat. Yara is
roused, the prince of thieves has lost his months of cunning, and this attempt
on the Heart is finished. The adventure ends here.

## E03 — Death in the Garden

Either the lion's noiseless rush reached your throat before strength, speed, or
luck could meet it, or your wounds have drained the last of your strength into
the lotus-scented grass. Taurus curses beneath his breath above you, then fades
back toward the wall; there is nothing left to save.

Above the dark garden the silver tower gleams cold beneath the stars, and the
Heart of the Elephant remains beyond your reach. Conan dies here, and the
adventure ends.
