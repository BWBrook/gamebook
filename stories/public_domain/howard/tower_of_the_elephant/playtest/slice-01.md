# *The Tower of the Elephant*: Outer Wall Playtest

Read the [player's rules](quick-rules.md) first. Then copy or print the
[Adventure Sheet](adventure-sheet.md), fill in Conan's starting values, and
begin at **S01**.

## S01 — The Outer Wall

Thick shrubbery hides you from the empty streets of the temple quarter. Above
it rises the outer wall of Yara's precinct, high enough to stop an ordinary
thief. You measure the pale stone with your eyes. A leap will put your fingers
on the coping; from there your own strength will do the rest.

Beyond that wall stands a second, and beyond the second gleams the silver
tower. Its jewel-crusted rim catches the starlight. No lamp burns anywhere in
its smooth height. Men say Yara has lived for centuries by the power of the
Heart of the Elephant. They tell, too, how he laughed in the face of a hostile
prince and held up a glowing gem, and how the prince shrivelled into a black
spider that Yara crushed beneath his heel. Such tales are the breath of
cowards, yet the hair along your neck rises.

Then a measured tread sounds from the garden within. Steel gives a faint
clink. So a guard does pace those gardens after all.

- Climb now, while you know where the guard is. Turn to **S03**.

- Press against the stone and wait for him to pass again. Add **1 Exposure**,
  then turn to **S02**.

## S02 — The Broken Round

You wait, counting the guard's round by the beat of your own blood. He should
pass again soon.

He does not. The silence stretches until you are certain of it. Somewhere
beyond the wall a fountain tinkles, and nothing else moves. A patrol does not
simply stop. Either the man has left his post, or something in that garden has
stopped him, and stopped him quietly.

You have spent time that may be missed, but you cross that wall knowing what
an impatient thief would not: whatever waits inside kills without a sound.
Write **Warned by the silence** under Story marks. Then leap for the coping
and turn to **S03**.

## S03 — The Dead Guard

You catch the coping, swing up with one arm, and lie flat on the broad stone.
Starlight lies over the even sward between the walls. Trimmed bushes stand
near the inner barrier; the small fountain is the only thing that speaks.

You lower yourself inside, draw your sword, and run crouched through the naked
light toward the bushes. A crumpled body almost takes your feet from under
you. The man wears the silvered armour and crested helmet of the royal guard.
His shield and spear lie beside him. His flesh is still warm. This is the man
whose tread you heard, dead within moments and without a cry.

A branch shifts near the inner wall. Something heavy is moving behind the
leaves.

- Go after it now, before it can slip away or settle into a better position.
  You leave the corpse unread and the spear where it lies, but you will come
  upon the mover while its attention is still on the body you found. Turn to
  **S05**.

- Spend a moment on the corpse first. The mover will have time to hear you
  and ready itself, but the dead man may have something to give you. Turn to
  **S04**.

## S04 — One Discovery

The thing in the shrubs is still near. You have time to take one thing from
the dead man, no more. Choose one; do not record both.

- Take up the guard's spear. Its shaft is long enough to brace against a
  charging beast. Write **Guard's spear** under Possessions. Turn to **S05**.

- Leave the spear and read the body instead. The throat is bruised by powerful
  hands, and the way he has fallen shows a swift attack from behind: no
  sorcery, but a man's strength. Write **Read the strangling** under Story
  marks. Turn to **S05**.

## S05 — A Thief in the Shrubs

You move toward the inner wall. A huge human shape waits close against the
stone, quiet enough to have killed the guard and to have heard your own
careful approach. It has not yet shown its purpose.

**If you have Read the strangling**, the dead man has already given you the
stranger's measure: a man, a killer, and no soldier. You name him softly for
what he is, a thief, before either of you lunges. Cross off **Read the
strangling** and turn to **S06**.

Otherwise, choose one.

- Call low from the edge of the shrubs that you know a man is hiding there.
  The challenge forces an answer, but your voice carries across the open
  sward. Add **1 Exposure** and turn to **S06**.

- Stalk him. Glide through the leaves as silently as a hunting panther and
  come upon him from his blind side. Test **Cunning 12**. Roll with
  **Advantage** if you have **Warned by the silence**, or if you came here
  straight from **S03** without stopping at the corpse. Cross off the mark if
  you used it.

  *Success:* you reach him unseen. Turn to **S06**.

  *Failure:* he hears you and seizes the better position, and there is a
  brief, ugly scuffle before you have him at sword's point. Lose **1 Stamina**
  and add **1 Exposure**. Turn to **S06**.

  *Natural 1:* you are on him so completely that he checks his lunge before it
  begins. Turn to **S06**.

  *Natural 20:* he catches you at full disadvantage before either of you
  understands the other. Lose **2 Stamina** and add **2 Exposure**. Turn to
  **S06**.

## S06 — The Prince of Thieves

The shape wheels with startling speed. A thick pair of hands starts forward,
then halts when your blade catches the starlight. The man is as tall as you
and heavier, big-bellied yet balanced like a wrestler. A thin, knotted rope
hangs coiled across him. His bare feet make no sound.

He sees that you wear no royal silver. You see that he is no soldier.

“You are no soldier,” he hisses. “You are a thief like myself.”

“Name yourself,” you answer, keeping the sword between you.

He is Taurus of Nemedia. You know the name: thieves speak it with the respect
princes demand of courtiers. When you give your own and say that you have come
for Yara's jewel, his belly shakes with a low laugh, and there is no mockery
in it. He had thought himself the only man alive with the courage for this
theft. He has laid his plans for months; you, he says, have acted on a sudden
impulse. Each of you recognises in the other something rarer than caution.

You ask whether he killed the guard. He admits it readily. He slid over the
wall while the man was on the far side of the garden, hid in the bushes, and
when the fool came blundering over, got behind him and choked out his life.

“You made one mistake,” you say. “You should have dragged the body into the
bushes.”

Anger flashes in his eyes. Then he gives you the harder calculation. The guard
will not be changed until past midnight. Should anyone come searching and find
the body, they will run bellowing to Yara and give the thieves time to escape.
Should they find nothing, they will beat through every bush and catch the
intruders like rats in a trap.

“You are right,” you agree.

Taurus's pride settles. There are no human guards in the inner garden, he
says, though there are sentinels more deadly than men, and he has found their
answer. Turn to **S07**.

## S07 — A Bargain Between Thieves

Taurus has never shared an adventure with anyone, but by Bel, god of thieves,
he will share this one. He means to enter from the top of the tower, come down
upon Yara, and strangle the old sorcerer before he can cast a spell. He
promises nothing. It is the chance of being turned into a spider or a toad,
he says, against the wealth and power of the world, and a good thief must
know how to take risks.

- Go as far as any man. Accept Taurus as an ally and turn to **S08**.

- Leave this theft to the man who planned it, while the outer wall is still
  at your back. Turn to **E01**.

## S08 — The Inner Wall

“I'll go as far as any man,” you tell him, and slip off your sandals.

Taurus turns to the inner wall, leaps, catches the coping, and draws his great
bulk up with a suppleness that would shame a lean acrobat. You follow, and the
two of you lie flat on the broad top, whispering.

You see no light. The lower tower is a perfect gleaming cylinder without a
visible opening. Its doors and windows are cleverly made and closed, Taurus
says; the soldiers inside breathe air that comes from above.

Below you the garden is a vague pool of shadow where feathery bushes and low
trees stir in the starlight. Something old in your blood catches a scent, and
the short hairs on your neck bristle as a hound's do at an ancient enemy.

Drop down after Taurus. Turn to **S09**.

## S09 — Eyes in the Garden

Taurus draws a slender copper tube from his girdle. “Keep behind me,” he
whispers, “as you value your life.” He drops lightly to the sward. You are
close behind him with your sword ready, but he pushes you back against the
wall and waits, his gaze fixed on the shrubbery a few yards away.

The shrubbery shakes, though the breeze has died. Two great eyes blaze from
the shadows; behind them other sparks kindle.

“Lions,” you mutter.

“Aye. By day they are kept in caverns below the tower. That is why there are
no guards in this garden.”

You count five pairs of eyes, and there may be more back in the bushes. Low
rumblings rise from the shadows. The eyes move forward. Your hand tightens on
the hilt, and you wait for the rush of great bodies.

Hold where Taurus put you and give him room. Turn to **S10**.

## S10 — The Black Lotus

Taurus steps out from the wall as if treading on razors and lifts the tube to
his lips. He blows, hard. A long jet of yellowish powder shoots from the far
end and billows into a thick green-yellow cloud that settles over the
shrubbery and blots out the glaring eyes. He runs back to the wall.

From the cloud comes no sound at all.

“What is that mist?” you ask.

“Death. If a wind rises and blows it back on us we go over the wall. But the
air is still. Wait until it is gone entirely. To breathe it is death.”

So you wait, and the waiting is its own trial. If your Exposure is 3 or
higher, a door grates somewhere beyond the outer wall while you stand there,
and Taurus's head turns toward it: the watch has begun to wonder about its
missing man.

At last only yellow shreds hang ghostly in the air, and then they too are
gone. Taurus motions you forward. Among the bushes lie five great tawny
shapes, the fire of their eyes dimmed for ever. A sweet, cloying scent hangs
over them. The powder was made from the black lotus of far Khitai, Taurus
tells you, and that one charge was all he had. Stealing it was a feat that
would have made his name by itself; there is none left for the soldiers.

Follow him to the foot of the tower. Turn to **S11**.

## S11 — The Lion That Made No Sound

You glide through the shrubbery to the gleaming foot of the tower. Taurus
unwinds his knotted cord, a steel hook at one end, and begins to swing it
about his head. You lay your ear to the smooth wall and hear nothing. The
soldiers inside do not yet know you are here.

Then your savage instinct tears you around, because the death that is upon
you makes no sound. A sixth lion rears upright against the stars, towering
over you for the killing stroke. It escaped the cloud, and it neither roars
nor growls as it comes.

It is already close enough to kill. Whatever you choose, a natural 20 is
death, and Fortune cannot touch it. On an ordinary roll you may spend Fortune
as usual.

- **Brace the guard's spear.** Only if you have **Guard's spear**. Cross it
  off now; the brace commits the weapon whatever follows. Test **Brawn 13
  with Advantage**.

  *Success:* the lion dies on the point. Turn to **S12**.

  *Failure:* the shaft snaps and the beast comes through it. Lose **2
  Stamina**, add **1 Exposure**, and turn to **S13**.

  *Natural 1:* it dies before it can make a sound. Turn to **S12**.

  *Natural 20:* the point skates from its skull and its whole weight comes
  down on you. Turn to **E03**.

- **Meet the leap with your sword.** Put every ounce of nerve and thew behind
  one stroke. Test **Brawn 13**.

  *Success:* your blade splits its skull and man and beast go down together.
  You climb out from under it. Turn to **S12**.

  *Failure:* the stroke lands, but so does the lion. Lose **2 Stamina**, add
  **1 Exposure**, and turn to **S13**.

  *Natural 1:* the skull is split before the beast has finished rising. Turn
  to **S12**.

  *Natural 20:* the lion is quicker. Turn to **E03**.

- **Slip aside and cut as it passes.** Let its own speed carry it past your
  blade. Test **Agility 12**.

  *Success:* it dies in the shrubs behind you. Turn to **S12**.

  *Failure:* a paw catches you as it goes by and the two of you crash through
  the bushes together. Lose **1 Stamina**, add **2 Exposure**, and turn to
  **S13**.

  *Natural 1:* you turn its speed against it. Turn to **S12**.

  *Natural 20:* it is not fooled. It bears you down. Turn to **E03**.

## S12 — The Clean Kill

The beast came without a sound, but your stroke was quicker. Tawny weight and
driving muscle collapse beside you, and the lion dies before it can voice an
alarm.

Taurus stares from the rope to the carcass and then to you. For a moment the
prince of thieves has nothing to say. He has seen quick killers in every
thieves' quarter from Nemedia to Zamora, and he has never seen a man move as
you just moved.

“Are you hurt?”

“No, by Crom.” If you braced the spear, its shaft lies snapped or its head
buried past recovery; leave it. If you used the sword, you wrench it free and
cleanse it on the grass.

Give Taurus room to finish the cast. Turn to **S14**.

## S13 — Victory at a Cost

The lion dies under your final stroke, but not cleanly and not quietly. Claws
or crushing weight have torn you before the carcass fell, and the struggle
has scuffed the garden's stillness. Taurus hauls the dead weight off you while
you force yourself upright.

Check your sheet. If your Stamina is 0, turn to **E03**. If your Exposure is
5, turn to **E02**.

Otherwise, Taurus asks if you are hurt, and you tell him it was as close a
call as you have had in a life noways tame. He glances up at the dark bulk of
the tower. Little sound was made, but the soldiers might have heard if they
are not asleep or drunk. Ready your sword and give him room to cast. Turn to
**S14**.

## S14 — The Rope Holds

Taurus resumes the interrupted swing and releases the hook with a smooth,
ripping motion of his mighty arm. Steel curves up and inward and vanishes over
the jewelled rim. He jerks the line cautiously, then hauls on it with his full
weight. Nothing slips.

“Luck the first cast,” he murmurs.

The silent lion lies dead. The five others lie in the lotus-scented shadows.
If your Exposure is 3 or higher, a voice is raised somewhere beyond the outer
wall and cut short; the watch is looking for its missing man, and the two of
you have less of the night than you had. Taurus says the cord will bear three
times his weight, and he will go first.

**This part of the adventure is complete.** The story continues up the rope,
but that continuation is not printed here. Keep your Adventure Sheet exactly
as it stands, including your Exposure, any possession you still hold, and any
story mark you have not crossed off. Nothing recovers, and nothing is erased.

## E01 — The Theft Abandoned

Taurus shrugs and turns to his inner wall without a backward look. You cross
back the way you came and drop into the temple-quarter shadows. Pride stings,
but no jewel is worth walking another man's road into a sorcerer's tower.

You are alive, and the Heart of the Elephant remains where it was. Whether
Taurus reaches it, you will not learn tonight. The adventure ends here.

## E02 — The Breach Discovered

Voices answer the evidence you have left behind. Steel sounds inside the
lower tower and beyond the garden wall, and the news will run to Yara, just
as Taurus said it would. With the lotus powder spent and the upper route
known, no quiet way into the tower remains.

You win clear over the walls while there is still time to do so. Yara is
warned, the prince of thieves has lost his months of planning, and this
attempt on the Heart is finished. The adventure ends here.

## E03 — Death in the Garden

Either the lion's noiseless rush reached your throat before strength, speed,
or luck could answer it, or your wounds have taken the last of your strength
in the lotus-scented dark. Taurus curses beneath his breath and bends over
you, but there is nothing left to help.

The tower stands above the dark garden, and the Heart of the Elephant remains
beyond your reach. Conan dies here, and the adventure ends.
