### Exposure

Exposure measures the growing risk that Conan's intrusion will be noticed.
Circle the current value; begin at **0**.

**0** | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5

0–2 — Hidden; 3–4 — The watch is looking; 5 — Discovered.
